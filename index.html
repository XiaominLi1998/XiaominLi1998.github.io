About Me

Industry I am a Senior Research Scientist at Google DeepMind (LLM post-training, security, and autonomous agents). Before that, I was a Senior Research Scientist at Microsoft CoreAI (mid-training, post-training, coding, agent RL, personalization, and agent harnesses). I held research internships at Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, and ByteDance.

Ph.D. I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from Harvard University, advised by Prof. Yue M. Lu, and I am also a research associate at MIT. My research connects high-dimensional information processing with practical problems in large language models, including data quality, alignment, interpretability, reasoning, uncertainty, and hallucination detection.

Undergraduate I earned dual bachelor’s degrees in Mathematics and Computer Science from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and completed 10+ graduate-level courses. My undergraduate research spanned Number Theory, Analytic Number Theory, Lie Algebra, Cohomology, Virtual Reality, Recommender Systems, and Computer Visualization.

MatrAIx I organize MatrAIx, an open-source simulated-user evaluation infrastructure for digital products and AI systems powered by 8.3 billion persona agents. Since launch, MatrAIx has generated 5,000+ posts on X within 10 hours, been featured twice in Forbes, received an invitation from Nature senior editors, and attracted multiple media interviews. The project has also led to invited talks at Stanford, Columbia, NVIDIA, Amazon, Adobe, Microsoft, Netflix, and Google DeepMind.
